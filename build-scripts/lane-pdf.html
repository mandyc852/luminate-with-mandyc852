A WEEKLY INQUIRY

LANE

The loop runs whether you watch it or not. Most weeks look like last week. LANE is the inquiry that makes the loop visible enough to choose, this week, what to repeat and what to change.

Once a week. Forty-five minutes. Pen and paper.

MANDYC.

THE QUESTIONS

L Lookback

What happened this week

  • Did I complete what I set out to do?
  • Does my calendar match my values?
  • What was my split between $10/hour work and $10,000/hour work?
  • Which tasks gave me leverage, and which were noise?
  • What past pattern showed up this week?
  • Where did I spend time on autopilot?
A Anchor

What I'm holding now

  • What worked well?
  • Where did I get stuck?
  • What did I learn?
  • Did I show up for the people who matter to me?
  • When did I feel most energized this week?
  • What's on my Not-Do list?
E Envision

The bigger picture

  • If I could only work two hours a week on this, what would I do?
  • What decisions could I let go of up to $100? $500? $1,000?
  • What can I subtract instead of add to solve this?
  • Am I working on the lever or the noise?
  • What's the easy version of this I'm not seeing?
  • Who would I have to become to make my next chapter real?
  • If I were already that person, what would I do this week?
N Next

What's coming

  • What's in my head that should be on paper?
  • Which projects don't have a next action?
  • What am I waiting on that needs a follow-up?
  • What's on my calendar this week that needs preparation?
  • What's in my Someday pile that's actually now?
  • What old tasks need to be killed, delegated, or finally done?

The framework is the discipline. The questions are the prompts. The answers are between you and the page.
